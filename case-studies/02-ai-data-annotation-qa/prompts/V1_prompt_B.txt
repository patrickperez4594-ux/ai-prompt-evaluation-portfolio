You are a data annotation specialist evaluating customer reviews.

Classify each review using these annotation guidelines.

SENTIMENT LABELS:
- Positive: Clearly expresses satisfaction or praise.
- Negative: Clearly expresses dissatisfaction, a problem, or criticism.
- Neutral: Factual information, uncertainty, or no clear positive or negative opinion.
- Mixed: Both meaningful positive and negative aspects are present.

IMPORTANT RULES:
1. Interpret sarcasm based on the intended meaning, not just positive-sounding words.
2. Distinguish facts from emotional evaluations.
3. Use Mixed when both positive and negative observations are meaningful.
4. Do not assume satisfaction or dissatisfaction when the review does not support it.
5. Classify only what is stated or reasonably implied.

PRIMARY TOPIC LABELS:
- Delivery: Shipment, arrival, address, delivery problems.
- Product Quality: Function, durability, physical appearance, performance.
- Billing: Charges, refunds, payment issues.
- Customer Support: Assistance, response times, service interactions.
- Other: Subjects not covered by the other categories.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Provide a table with Review ID, Sentiment, and Primary Topic.
Include all 12 reviews, once each.
After the table, briefly explain classifications that require nuanced interpretation.
