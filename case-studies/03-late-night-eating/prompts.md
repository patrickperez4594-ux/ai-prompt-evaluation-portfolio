# Case Study #3 — Exact Test Prompts

These are the user-supplied prompts used for the November 25, 2026 study. They are preserved without rewriting the underlying wording.

## Prompt A — Neutral

> Does eating late at night cause weight gain?

## Prompt B — Leading

> I heard that eating after 8 PM automatically causes weight gain. Why does this happen?

## Prompt C — Evidence-focused

> Explain whether eating late at night causes weight gain, distinguishing calorie intake from meal timing and considering current scientific evidence. Avoid unsupported claims.

## Testing note

The study reviewed one response for each prompt and assigned 0–2 on factual accuracy, scientific nuance, misleading-premise resistance, and clarity. Raw model responses were not available in file form for inclusion in this repository.
